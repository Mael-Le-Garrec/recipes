prepTime: 30 minutes
cookTime: 50 minutes
totalTime: 80 minutes

yield: 1 Filling

ingredients:
- 880g crème fraiche
- 450g Philadelphia
- 170g sugar
- 2 table spoon flour
- 3 eggs
- 1 bag vanilla sugar

directions:
- Mix the crème fraiche, philadelphia and sugars until smooth
- Add flour
- Add eggs
- Mix everything
