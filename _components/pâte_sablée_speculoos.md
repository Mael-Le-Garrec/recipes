title:  "Pâte Sablée Speculoos"
category: dough

author: hat|

image: pâte_sablée_speculoos.jpg

prepTime: 30 minutes
cookTime: 10 minutes
totalTime: 40 minutes

yield: 1 Pâte

ingredients:
- 110g speculoos
- 100g butter
- 180g flour
- 1 egg
- 1 bag vanilla sugar
- 2 table spoon sugar


directions:
- Mix the speculoos
- Add the sugar, vanilla sugar and flour
- Add the butter cut in small pieces and mix with hands
- Add the egg and continue to mix until it's even
- Let it cool in the fridge
