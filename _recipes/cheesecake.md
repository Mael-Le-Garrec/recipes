prepTime: 40 minutes
cookTime: 60 minutes
totalTime: 120 minutes

yield: 1 Cheesecake

components:
- Pâte Sablée Speculoos
- Cheesecake Filling

ingredients:
- Butter

directions:
- Butter a ø25.5cm mold
- Prepare the dough and distribute it evenly in the mold
- Spike the dough
- Bake it at 180°C for 10 minutes
- Prepare the filling and pour it on top of the dough
- Bake for 50 minutes at 180°C

further_improvements:
- Cooking for longer makes it rise too much

---

New-York style cheesecake. Quite heavy but nice :)
