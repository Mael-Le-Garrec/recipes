prepTime: 20 minutes
cookTime: 70 minutes
totalTime: 90 minutes

yield: 1 Pie

ingredients:
- 60cl whole milk
- 250g sugar
- 160g flour
- 100g butter
- 1 bag vanilla sugar
- 8 eggs
- 5 apples
- Cinnamon

directions:
- Butter the mold and put some flour on it
- Cut the apples in small bits and place them in the mold
- Melt the butter
- Mix the eggs, sugar, and 1 bag of vanilla until it becomes white
- Add butter
- Add flour
- Add milk
- Pour the dough on the apples
- Bake at 200°C for roughly 1h
- Put cinnamon on top at the end

further_improvements:
- Seems good for now!

An apple clafoutis, except something very heavy.
Quick easy to make, the only annoying part is to cut the apples.
