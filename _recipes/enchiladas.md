prepTime: 40 minutes
cookTime: 15 minutes
totalTime: 55 minutes

yield: 1 tray

ingredients:
- 400g crushed tomatoes (gross)
- 400 white beans (gross)
- 800g black beans (gross)
- 200g corn (gross)
- 1 red onion
- 2 garlic cloves
- 6 tortilla
- Grated cheese
- 1 salsa pot
- 1 tsp oil
- 2 tsp tomato concentrate
- chili spices mix

directions:
- Pre-heat the oven to 225°C.
- Chop the onion and fry it in a pan with oil.
- Chop the garlic and add it to the pan.
- Add the cumin and chili powder to it.
- Add the tomato concentrate and the crushed tomatoes. Wait for it to dry a bit.
- Add salt and pepper.
- Rince the beans and add them to the pan.
- Add the corn
- Fill the tortillas with the preparation and roll them on an oven tray.
- Put cheese on top.
- Bake for 15 minutes.

Vegetarian enchiladas with bought salsa. Really tasty, easy and quick to make!
