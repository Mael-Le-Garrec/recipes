prepTime: 20 minutes
cookTime: 20 minutes
totalTime: 40 minutes

yield: 1 Cake

ingredients:
- 100g flour
- 100g sugar
- 3 eggs
- 1/2 bag of baking powder

directions:
- Mix sugar and eggs
- Put them in a water bath and whip until you obtain a creamy texture, i.e. it shouldn't be liquid but not hard either. The temperature shouldn't exceed 45°C.
- Take off of the water bath
- Add the sifted flour and baking power, fold with a spatula
- Butter the mold
- Put the dough in the mold
- Bake at 180°C for roughly 20 minutes

---

An easy to do sponge cake, mainly used for layer cakes but good on its own!  
This recipe uses a mold of 18cm diameter.
